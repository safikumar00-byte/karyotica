GROW FAST

On-Stim (14-0-0) is a powerful, water-soluble amino acid product derived from enzyme digestion of non-GM soybean protein. It provides plants with readily available nitrogen and carbon, saving them energy and improving growth efficiency. On-Stim’s unique formulation is perfect for a variety of agricultural and horticultural applications, delivering consistent results across diverse environments.

  • High Nitrogen Content (14% Available Nitrogen): On-Stim’s high nitrogen content ensures optimal plant health, enhancing growth and productivity.
  • Amino Acid-Enhanced Nutrient Delivery: The amino acids in On-Stim are easily absorbed and utilized by plants, providing a direct source of essential nutrients.
  • Versatile Application: On-Stim can be used in foliar feeding, fertigation, drip irrigation, hydroponics, and aquaponics systems, making it adaptable to both traditional and modern farming setups.
  • Eco-Friendly & Sustainable: Derived from upcycled organic materials, On-Stim helps reduce reliance on synthetic fertilizers, contributing to more sustainable farming practices.
  • Appearance: light brown powder. Total amino acid: 80% min. Heavy metals:10 ppm max. As:5 ppm max, NPK:14- 0-0, CAS# 68607-88-5 and 68308-36-1

Increased Plant Growth

Faster, healthier growth with improved nutrient absorption.

Enhanced Plant Resilience

On-Stim helps plants thrive in both normal and challenging conditions, supporting resilience against environmental stress.

Reduced Input Costs

A lower need for additional fertilizers and nutrients, leading to cost savings.

Lower Maintenance

With optimized nutrient delivery, On-Stim reduces the need for constant care and monitoring.

Application Rates and Key Features

  • Greenhouse and garden plants: 1tsp per 4 liters of water
  • Other plants: 30gm to 400gm per 200 liters of water Drench and foliar, see product label for details.
  • Registrations and Licensing Available for sale in North America
  • Derived from: NON-GMO Soybean
  • Stimulates nutrient uptake and enhances chlorophyll content in leaves
  • Is 100% water soluble and available for quick absorption in plants
  • Improves flavors, firmness, and preservation of yield
  • Can be mixed with fertilizers to their function with each other.
  • No toxicity known
