Who We Are

About Karyotica Labs

Karyotica Labs is a Canadian based company at the forefront of sustainable agriculture. Our mission is to transform organic byproducts from agriculture into cutting-edge organic plant-growth enhancements (aka -biostimulants) using advanced enzyme-associated microbial fermentation. By leveraging the power of industrial enzymes and beneficial microbes, we create innovative products that enhance plant growth, resilience, and productivity while reducing the need for chemical inputs.

With a deep commitment to eco-friendly practices, we address global challenges such as rising agricultural costs, climate stress, and the growing demand for sustainable farming solutions.

Our Story

Empowering Growers for a Better Tomorrow

Karyotica Labs is dedicated to advancing sustainable agriculture through novel, eco-friendly biostimulants, empowering GROWERS with cost-effective solutions that enhance plant growth, resilience, and sustainability while reducing environmental impact.

Building a Sustainable Agricultural Future

We envision a future where agriculture flourishes in balance with nature. Our innovative solutions drive sustainable practices, enabling growers to meet global food demands while conserving vital natural resources. By transforming agricultural byproducts into high-value biostimulants, we are fostering a healthier planet and building a more resilient, sustainable agricultural sector.

From Vision to Reality

Founded in 2024, Karyotica Labs was born from a desire to address the growing challenges in modern agriculture. Recognizing the unsustainable nature of traditional farming practices, we set out to create eco-friendly alternatives that could revolutionize the way the world grows food.

Today, we proudly serve the agricultural community with innovative biostimulants that support sustainable practices, improve crop yields, and reduce environmental impact. Our flagship products, such as Thrive and Grow Fast, are a testament to our commitment to driving positive change in agriculture.
