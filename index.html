Hemp Carbon Standards
THRIVE Biostimulant

“Karyotica Labs has truly set a new standard for agricultural innovation with their signature biostimulant, THRIVE. In 2023, THRIVE revolutionized hemp farming in Nova Scotia, helping growers achieve an astonishing 10.4 metric tons (MT) of carbon sequestered per hectare, even under suboptimal growing conditions marked by drought and flooding. This remarkable achievement was validated through an international comparative study involving farms across the UK, USA, Spain, Western Ukraine, and Canada. The results from Nova Scotia stood out thanks to THRIVE's unparalleled ability to boost plant resilience and optimize growth. By treating hemp seeds with THRIVE, farmers witnessed germination and soil emergence in just three days, forming a rapid canopy that gave the plants a crucial head start. This early advantage led to taller plants, greater biomass production, and significantly enhanced resilience to environmental stressors. The rapid growth was a key driver in the increased carbon storage, proving THRIVE’s efficacy in both crop productivity and environmental impact. Karyotica Labs’ products, particularly THRIVE, are essential additions to modern farming practices, supporting the growth of hemp and a variety of agricultural and horticultural crops while acting as a form of crop insurance against challenging growing conditions. We are incredibly excited about the future of carbon sequestration in farm crops facilitated by THRIVE and other Karyotica products. This advancement holds immense potential for boosting carbon credit revenues, benefiting farmers and the planet alike. THRIVE has undoubtedly proven itself as a game-changer, and we look forward to seeing its continued impact on sustainable agriculture.”
